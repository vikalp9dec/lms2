How soon will I see results?

Paid ads can generate leads within the first 1–2 weeks. SEO and content typically show meaningful growth in 3–6 months. I'll set realistic expectations on our first call.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Monthly plans are month-to-month with 30 days' notice. One-off projects are quoted upfront.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No — ad spend is paid directly to Google/Meta from your own account, so you stay in full control. Package prices cover management and strategy.

Which industries do you work with?

Local services, clinics, e-commerce, coaches, real estate, restaurants, SaaS and B2B companies. If you're unsure, just ask.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide and schedule calls in your time zone.
